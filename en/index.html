FAQ

What couples ask before booking

How much does a wedding DJ cost?

The price depends on the date, the location and the nature of the event. A wedding DJ doesn't have to cost ₪13,000. Get in touch for a quote with a website discount.

Who is DJ Amithigh?

Guy Amithigh, also known as DJ Amithigh, is a Tel Aviv DJ who plays weddings and business events all over Israel. He starts with a conversation about the event you imagined and builds the evening around your taste.

Which events can I book you for?

Weddings, business events, private events and boutique events.

Which music styles do you play?

Mainstream, house, techno, disco, Greek, Latin, reggaeton and the hits of the 90s and 2000s. The set is tailored to the crowd and the event.

How do I check if my date is free?

Send me a WhatsApp message or call 052-5353259 with the date and location, or fill in the date-check form on the site. I'll get back to you with an answer and details.

How far in advance should I book?

Most couples book between six months and a year ahead. When the date is free, you can also book three months before the event.

Which areas do you play in?

All over Israel. Based in Tel Aviv.
